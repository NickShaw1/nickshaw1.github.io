Tokyo, Mt Fuji, Kyoto, Nara and Hiroshima, all in a few weeks. A genuinely hard trip to follow.

I recently set up a new PC, so this post is partly a test to confirm everything (GitHub, Node.js and deployment processes) is configured correctly on the new machine. After reinstalling Node.js and re-establishing my GitHub setup, everything appears to be running smoothly. Looking forward to getting back to regular updates soon.

I also have a new training course coming up, which should provide some good material for future posts.
