One thing I have wanted to build for a while is a proper reference section for software testing. Not another list of links, but something structured and readable that covers the subject in depth. That is now live as the [Knowledge Base](/knowledge-base).

## What it is

The Knowledge Base is a wiki-style reference covering software testing from the ground up. It is organised into three areas.

**Theory** is the largest section. It covers foundations, manual testing, test management, automation, specialist testing, observability, AI and modern testing, tooling and concepts. Each section runs to several articles and covers both the principles and the practical application. The full theory section is around 390 minutes of reading, which reflects the scope of the subject rather than any intention to be exhaustive.

**Guides** will be practical, tool-specific walkthroughs. The first, covering Playwright, is nearly ready and will be published shortly.

**Reference** is for quick lookups: a glossary of testing terms, a pre-release testing checklist and a curated resource list covering certifications, courses and communities.

## The checklist

The testing checklist is worth a mention separately. It is an interactive tool with 30 items across six categories: functional, UI and compatibility, accessibility, performance, security and pre-release sign-off. You can work through it, track your progress and export your results to Excel for sign-off or audit evidence. It is designed to be genuinely useful in a real release process rather than a theoretical exercise.

## Why it exists

There are two reasons.

The first is practical. I spend a lot of time explaining testing concepts to developers, product owners and stakeholders. Having a reliable reference I can point people to saves time and keeps explanations consistent.

The second is that writing about something is one of the better ways to understand it properly. Building this has been a useful audit of what I actually know versus what I assumed I knew.

## What is next

The Guides section will grow as I add Playwright content. The theory sections will be expanded and updated as the discipline evolves, particularly around AI-assisted testing and quality engineering in agentic delivery contexts.

If something is missing, unclear or wrong, the site has a contact section. Feedback is welcome.
